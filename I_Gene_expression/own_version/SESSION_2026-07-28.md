### UMI saturation is real and cell-size-dependent

`LEN_UMI=6` → K = 4096 distinct UMIs per gene. Measured per cell:

| cell | genes >1000 UMIs | >2048 | ≥K | share of molecules in genes >1000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 007 | 132 | 36 | 4 | 14.7% |
| 010 | 250 | 57 | 8 | 18.8% |
| 011 | 242 | 56 | 9 | 18.6% |
| 002 | 27 | 16 | 2 | 11.0% |
| 001 (blank) | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1.9% |

Up to **18.8%** of a deep cell's molecules sit in genes that have consumed >24%
of UMI space. Use `*.TranscriptCounts.tsv` (collision-corrected,
`t = ln(1−x/K)/ln(1−1/K)`) for anything highly expressed, never `UFICounts`.
The 4–9 genes per deep cell at `x ≥ K` are clamped to a constant ~62,000 and
carry no information at all — drop them rather than interpret them.

This is protocol-inherent (CEL-seq2/VASA 6 nt UMI), not a defect of this fork.

### The first attempt failed, and it was my wrapper, not the pipeline

Job `50910834` FAILED with exit 2 after **0 seconds**, having written nothing.
Worth recording precisely, because "step 7 exited 2" is exactly what the run-1
`reduceGeneName` disaster also looked like from the outside, and it is not
what happened.

The cause was a line I had added to my own job wrapper as a *diagnostic*:

```bash
set -euo pipefail
...
ls -1 "$OUT"/ZHA9292A1*.tsv 2>/dev/null | wc -l     # <- kills the job
```

With no tables yet on disk the glob matches nothing, `ls` exits **2**,
`pipefail` promotes that exit through `wc`, and `set -e` aborts the script.
It printed its `0` and died — **before `pipeline.sh` was ever invoked**.
Confirmed by the job's own stdout, which ends after the count, and by
`bash -n` passing on the script.

Nothing in `$OUTDIR` was touched, so there was nothing to clean up.

Fix: count with `find`, which exits 0 on no match.

```bash
echo "tsv BEFORE (expect 0): $(find "$OUT" -maxdepth 1 -name 'ZHA9292A1*.tsv' | wc -l)"
```

The general trap, for anyone writing a wrapper around this pipeline: under
`set -euo pipefail`, a bare `ls` on a not-yet-existing output is a **job-killing
statement**, and `2>/dev/null` hides the reason. It is the same shape of problem
as the bare-`gzip` hazard already documented in `pipeline.sh` — a
non-zero exit from a command nobody thought of as fallible.

### Which table to use

Three flavours of every table exist and they are **not** interchangeable:

- **`*.TranscriptCounts.tsv`** — UFIs with the collision correction
  `t = ln(1−x/K)/ln(1−1/K)`, K = 4096. **This is the expression estimate.**
- **`*.UFICounts.tsv`** — raw molecule counts. Saturates. Use for detection
  (is this gene present?) and for QC, not for magnitude.
- **`*.ReadCounts.tsv`** — reads. Bookkeeping only.

For most analyses the entry point is
**`ZHA9292A1_uniaggGenes_total.TranscriptCounts.tsv`** (222,421 × 16), with
`_spliced` / `_unspliced` alongside it for velocity. The
`shortGeneNames_*` variants collapse to bare symbols and lose the Ensembl id.
